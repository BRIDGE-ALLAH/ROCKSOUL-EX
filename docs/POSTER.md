# ROCKSOUL-EX — Poster Concept 001

## Core idea

**A repository has a story. ROCKSOUL helps keep its direction.**

The poster visualizes the product as an engineering witness rather than a human mascot or a generic AI robot.

## Visual elements

- **Faceless monolith:** the Witness archetype—present, observant, and deliberately non-human.
- **Orbit / measurement rings:** context, inspection boundaries, and the act of mapping a project.
- **Graphite field:** focus and restraint.
- **Warm paper white:** editorial typography and human-readable intent.
- **Signal orange:** active mission and action.
- **Technical cyan:** observation and system signals.
- **Grid / registration marks:** structure, evidence, and precision.

## Poster copy

**ROCKSOUL-EX**  
THE ENGINEERING EXPERIENCE

A COMPANION FOR THE WHOLE ENGINEERING JOURNEY.

## Usage

The SVG in assets/rocksoul-ex-poster.svg is the editable source of Poster 001. It can be embedded in the repository README and used as a direction reference for future extension UI.

The poster is a campaign visual, not a literal screenshot of a working extension. Do not present it as an implemented interface.
